# Legal Source Registry

Canonical legal records use official sources and record authority class, jurisdiction, language, status, publication or adoption date, effective date, retrieval date, review date, supersession, and qualified-review status.

This registry is an applicability and evidence aid. It is not legal advice and it does not certify compliance.

A source may enter an active jurisdiction overlay only after its authority, status, scope, effective date, and control mapping are reviewed. Translations are labeled and never replace authoritative original-language text.
