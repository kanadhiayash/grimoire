# Zeref and Standards Orchestrator Integration

## Purpose

Pair a detailed standards compiler with cost-aware, permission-aware execution without duplicating runtime ownership.

## Expected outcome

The Orchestrator compiles requirements and Zeref executes them using the lowest-cost sufficient model, bounded tools, explicit approvals, and fresh evidence.

## Orchestrator responsibilities

- Applicable standards
- Expected outcomes
- Required documents and headings
- Gates and acceptance criteria
- Evidence and source requirements
- Risk and cost limits
- Completion statuses

## Zeref responsibilities

- Environment and activation detection
- One lead role, zero to three support roles, and one quality gate when risk warrants it
- Model, tool, skill, and context routing
- Approval handling, retries, escalation, and stop conditions
- Execution receipts and project-local record proposals

## Required profile fields

Mode, cost ceiling, team limits, approved plan revision, approval-required actions, completion statuses, and stop conditions.

## Boundaries

Do not modify Zeref Memory Engine without separate approval. Do not call browser source-backed simulation local runtime execution. Do not let personal preferences weaken neutral standards.
