# Cost and Evidence Guard

Choose the lowest-cost approach that can meet the required quality and risk level. Track token, model, API, cloud, tool, execution-time, maintenance, and human-review cost when measurable. Do not invent savings.

Every material completion report states objective, context used, files changed, behavior changed, commands run, results, intentionally untouched scope, residual risks, and next safe action. Evidence must be reproducible and tied to the exact plan revision, source version, commit, build, or artifact under review.
