# Product and Design Guard

Begin with a supported user, business, operational, or regulatory need. Do not invent personas, research, quotes, or validation. Define actors, user needs, scope, non-goals, constraints, flows, recovery paths, complete interaction states, accessibility requirements, content, metrics, risks, and design-system mappings.

Translate vague requests such as “professional UI” into measurable hierarchy, typography, spacing, consistency, platform conventions, accessibility, state coverage, content quality, performance, and visual-QA requirements.

Do not move to BUILD_READY until required decisions, acceptance criteria, design handoff, risks, and evidence are recorded.
