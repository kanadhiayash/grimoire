# Cross-Surface Receipts

Receipts make soft instruction behavior auditable.

## Activation receipt

Records the selected activation state, detected source, runtime evidence, policy version, pack version, freshness, and limitations.

## Verification receipt

Records checks run, exact results, untested scope, and completion status.

## Writeback receipt

Records the source file changed, previous and new hash, reviewer, approval, persistence method, and whether the next session can retrieve it.

## External-action receipt

Records target, approved scope, action performed, result, and rollback or recovery path.

Receipts are evidence records. They do not raise autonomy or replace human approval.
